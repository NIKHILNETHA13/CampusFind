How CampusFind Works

A simple way for students to report, find, and return lost items on campus.

Lost Something?

01

Report It

Report the lost item with its details, location, date, and an optional photo.

02

Students Find It

Other students can browse active lost and found items.

03

Someone Finds It

If another student finds your lost item, they can select "I Found It" and provide information.

04

Connect & Return

You and the interested student can use the provided contact information to coordinate the handover. Mark the item as RETURNED after it is received.

Found Something?

01

Report It

Post the found item with its details, location, date, and an optional photo.

02

Owner Finds It

The student who believes the item belongs to them can select "I Think It's Mine".

03

Share Information

The interested student can provide identifying information to help the reporter determine whether the item belongs to them.

04

Connect & Return

The reporter and interested student can communicate using the available contact information and arrange the handover. Mark the item as RETURNED after it is handed over.
