CampusFind

How CampusFind Works

A simple way for students to report lost and found belongings, discover possible matches, and connect with the right people.

CampusFind is a campus lost-and-found platform that helps students report belongings they have lost or found and makes it easier to connect with other students who may be able to help return them.

How It Works

01

Report an Item

Lost something or found something on campus? Create a report with the item's details, location, date, and an optional photo.

02

Make It Discoverable

Your report becomes visible to other students so they can look for possible lost or found items.

03

Find a Possible Match

Browse reported items and look for an item that matches something you lost or found.

04

Express Interest

For a LOST item: If you found the reported lost item, select "I Found It" and provide useful information.
For a FOUND item: If you believe a reported found item belongs to you, select "I Think It's Mine" and explain why.

05

Connect

The reporter and interested student can use the available contact information to communicate and discuss the item.
(CampusFind does NOT provide internal messaging.)

06

Return the Item

Arrange the handover between the students. Once the item has been received, the reporter can mark it as Returned.

What CampusFind Helps You Do

  • • Report lost belongings
  • • Report found belongings
  • • Browse possible matches
  • • Express interest in an item
  • • Receive notifications about relevant activity
  • • Connect with another student
  • • Track an item's status
  • • Mark an item as Returned after handover

What CampusFind Doesn't Do

  • • It does not automatically determine ownership.
  • • It does not approve or reject ownership claims.
  • • It does not provide internal messaging.
  • • It does not physically handle or deliver items.
  • • It does not guarantee that an interested student is the owner.

"CampusFind helps students connect. The students involved decide how to verify the item and arrange the handover."

Example

You lose your student ID somewhere on campus.

  1. You report it on CampusFind.
  2. Another student finds an ID and reports it.
  3. You discover the found-item report.
  4. You select "I Think It's Mine" and provide identifying information.
  5. The reporter reviews the information.
  6. You connect and arrange the handover.
  7. The item is marked Returned.
